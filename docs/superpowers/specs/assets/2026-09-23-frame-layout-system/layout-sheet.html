The system every layout shares

Grid12 columns and 16 rows inside a margin of 4% of the width. Text and photos start and end on column edges. Baselines and cap tops sit on row lines wherever a layout anchors them.

Three sizes, nothing betweenDisplay type is fitted to its space. Second level is 4.4% of the width, information is 1.95%. Information is also the minimum: the checker fails any text smaller than that.

Spacing belongs to the layoutDisplay 0.9 line spacing, −0.05 em letter spacing. Second level 1.04 and −0.02 em. Information 1.3 and 0. Font, weight and colour stay yours.

Measured, not guessedEvery block's height comes from lines × size × line spacing plus the font's real cap height, the same numbers the page draws with.

Photos reserve spaceA photo spans whole columns, keeps its proportions and claims its box. Type may only cross it where a layout says so on purpose (Photo behind, Full bleed).

One checker for every layoutThe same test runs on every tile, every title length, with and without a photo. In the real build it runs in the test suite across thousands of variations.
