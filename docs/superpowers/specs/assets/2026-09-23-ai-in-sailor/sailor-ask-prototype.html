Things to try
  1. The bottom bar is for making and asking: Select, Hand, Add, Studios, Generate, Actions, then the prompt. The libraries (Assets, Styles, Characters, Templates, and More with Toolbox, Nodes and Blocks) are in a rail on the left. Annotations moved into Add, beside the panels they open. Explain is gone.
  2. Select a node and the prompt reads “Ask about Rainy shop”. Click it or press /: it grows across the bar while you type, then shrinks back and shows progress while the results land on the work.
  3. Select Rainy shop and pick “make it rain on a window”. The takes appear just above the prompt, and the node glows. Hovering a take previews it on the node itself; nothing on the canvas moves or gets covered. Then Keep.
  4. The node toolbar is just Edit ▾ and Develop ▾. Items that need words (Remix…, Tune…, Restyle…) put a chip in the composer and wait for you.
  5. Shop front shows 2 fixes on the node. Click it for the fixes.
  6. Double-click Poster to open Frame. Frame's own tool bar is at the bottom, ending in the prompt. Select the headline, the background or nothing, and the prompt suggests different things: headlines, background effects or layouts. The takes land under the artboard.
  7. Click empty canvas and pick “add an upscale step before the poster”. The change appears on the canvas as a ghost, and you approve it in the composer.
  8. Double-click Rainy shop. The inspector opens with the layer's recipe (and Remix), then a plain list of actions. Takes land under the preview.
  9. Select Night version and type “add heavy rain to the prompt”. The instruction changes the prompt on the node, where you can see it.
