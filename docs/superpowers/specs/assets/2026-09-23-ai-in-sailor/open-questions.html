Six open questions

From the AI-in-Sailor spec. Each one shows the options side by side; the outlined one is my recommendation. Only the first has to be decided before building starts; the rest can wait until the stage that needs them.

1

Where do four takes go when there's a node right below?

Decide before stage 3

Takes hang under the node they belong to. On a busy canvas the strip lands on top of the next node down, which is exactly what we're trying to avoid.
